Creates the DTE 52 dispatch guide of a validated delivery.

A validated outgoing transfer gets a **Create Dispatch Guide** button
that opens a draft guide with the customer, the origin and destination
addresses and one line per delivered product with its done quantity. The
guide is then reviewed, frozen and issued to SII from the dispatch guide
form.

Configuration
=============

On the company, **Valued Dispatch Guides** decides whether guides
created from deliveries state prices. When enabled, each line takes the
price of its sale order line, or the product sales price, and is taxable
when the product bears VAT. When disabled, lines are unvalued.
