Chilean tax reports built on plain community accounting (no Enterprise
``account_reports``):

- **Balance de 8 columnas** (eight-column balance) for a company and a
  date range. For each account it shows debits, credits, debit balance,
  credit balance, assets, liabilities, losses and gains. Accounts are
  classified by their community account type: asset, liability and
  equity accounts go to the assets/liabilities columns and accumulate
  every posted entry up to the end date; income and expense accounts go
  to the losses/gains columns and only include the entries of the range.
  Results of earlier periods and the current-year earnings accounts are
  grouped in one *unallocated earnings* line, and a *profit/loss for the
  period* line balances both column pairs. It can be printed as PDF or
  opened as a list/pivot (exportable to spreadsheet with the standard
  export).
- **F29 worksheet** for a month: number of documents and VAT debits by
  document class (invoices, receipts, debit and credit notes, exempt
  documents and exports), VAT credits on purchases (recoverable, common
  use with the proportional factor, fixed assets, debit and credit notes
  received), the net amount without right to credit, VAT withheld on
  purchase invoices (total retention), second category withholdings, PPM
  and the resulting VAT payable or remaining credit for the next period.
  Each line shows the related SII F29 box codes.

Every F29 line is a configurable *F29 box* that reads the tax report
tags of the Chilean chart of accounts (``l10n_cl``) on tax or base
lines, or the untaxed amount of the documents, filtered by document
type.

Usage
=====

**Configuration**

1. In *Invoicing / Accounting > Configuration > Settings*, section
   *Chilean F29 Worksheet*, set the PPM rate and the proportional factor
   used for the common-use VAT credit (100% by default).

2. Optionally review *Configuration > Accounting > F29 Boxes*. The
   default boxes use the tax report tags of the ``l10n_cl`` chart:

   - VAT debit: ``IVA Debito Fiscal`` (documents 33/30, 39/35, 56/55,
     61/60).
   - Exempt documents (34/32/41/38) and exports (110/111/112): untaxed
     amount.
   - VAT credit: ``IVA Pagado Compras Recuperables``,
     ``IVA Pagado Compras Supermercado``,
     ``IVA Pagado Compras Uso Común`` (proportional),
     ``Compras Activo Fijo``, ``Compras Activo Fijo Uso Común``
     (proportional).
   - Without right to credit: base of ``Compras IVA No Recuperable`` and
     ``Compras de Activo Fijo No Recuperable``.
   - Withholdings: ``Retención Total (compras)`` (tax ``I_RTI``,
     purchase invoice 46) and ``Retención Segunda Categoría`` (fees).

   The community chart has no tax for exempt sales, PPM, the single tax
   on workers' wages or imports (DIN); create a box with your own tags
   or document types if you need them. Boxes may be restricted to one
   company.

**Usage**

- *Accounting > Reporting > Chile > F29 Worksheet*: choose the company,
  year and month, enter the credit remainder of the previous period and
  press *Compute*. Print the worksheet with *Print PDF*.
- *Accounting > Reporting > Chile > Eight-Column Balance*: choose the
  company and the date range, then *View* (list and pivot, exportable)
  or *Print PDF*.

Only posted entries are used. The F29 worksheet is an aid to fill the
form; check it against the Purchase and Sales Register (RCV) before
filing.
