Delivers accepted Chilean DTEs to the customer, as SII requires for the
exchange between taxpayers.

When SII accepts an invoice or note issued from Accounting, a queued job
emails the customer an ``EnvioDTE`` addressed to the customer's RUT and
the invoice PDF with the SII electronic stamp. Outside the Certification
and Production modes the signed DTE is attached instead of an envelope.

Usage
=====

1. On the customer, fill **DTE Exchange Email** with the mailbox the
   customer registered with SII. The partner email is used when it is
   empty.
2. Issue the DTE from the invoice. Once SII accepts it, the delivery is
   queued automatically. The invoice shows the delivery state and
   message.
3. Use **Send DTE to Customer** to resend it or to deliver manually.
