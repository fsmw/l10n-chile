Storage for the SII purchase and sales register (Registro de Compras y
Ventas, RCV), read-only download of the register from the SII, and
import of its detail CSV.

The RCV is kept by period (company, year, month and operation:
purchases, sales or receipt sales). Each read of a period, from the SII
or from a CSV file, is stored as a read with its coverage by SII state,
the summary totals by document type, and the fiscal documents it
reports. A document is identified by company, operation, document type,
issuer RUT and folio; every read updates it and keeps an observation
with the reported amounts, so the history of a document across reads is
preserved.

A section whose detail does not prove its summary (missing rows,
different counts or totals) is kept as partial. The absence of a
document is never inferred from an incomplete read.

The SII read signs in to the SII website with the company's production
signing certificate (mutual TLS) and calls, read-only, the services of
the RCV pages: the summary of each RCV state (registered, claimed and
pending for purchases; registered for sales) and the detail of each
document type. Reads run in the background, and companies can opt in to
a daily read of the current and previous month. Receipt sales (boletas)
cannot be read from the SII yet, because there is no validated SII
service for them.

Each stored document is reconciled with the journal entries of its
company. Purchases are matched with vendor bills and sales with customer
invoices by document type, folio and the RUT of the commercial partner;
for sales, our issued DTE with that type and folio is preferred (a DTE
rejected by the SII is ignored). The net, exempt, VAT and total amounts
and the accounting period are then compared, and the document is marked
as matched, missing in accounting, duplicate, with a period or amount
difference, for manual review or indeterminate, with a reason code and a
message. For purchases, the recoverable, non-recoverable and common use
VAT allocation is compared separately: it only qualifies a fiscal match.

A document whose RCV section was read partially or failed is never
reported as missing in accounting: it stays indeterminate. Likewise, a
journal entry of the period is reported as not in the RCV only when
every SII state of the operation (registered, claimed and pending for
purchases; registered for sales) was read completely; otherwise it is
marked as RCV incomplete. A CSV import never proves that coverage.

The reconciliation never writes journal entries. Every automatic
decision is recorded once in the reconciliation log with its evidence;
manual links and unlinks are recorded with their reason. The period
report (RCV Book) prints the summary and the documents of the current
read with their reconciliation state.

Usage
=====

DTE users read the register from DTE Issuance > Purchase and Sales
Register. *Periods* opens the periods of the current year, creating the
missing ones; *Open Fiscal Year* does the same for another year.

To read a period from the SII, a DTE Manager clicks *Read from SII* on
the period, or opens *Read from SII* and selects the company, operation
(purchases or sales), year and month. The company needs a valid RUT and
an active production signing profile (DTE Issuance > Configuration)
whose certificate has its private key and is authorized at the SII for
the company. The read is queued and processed in the background by the
*SII RCV: Process Queued Reads* scheduled action; only one read per
period is queued at a time. The result is stored as a new read of the
period, with its coverage by SII state. A failed read records the failed
step (sign-in, session token, RCV portal, session configuration, session
load, session validation, session parameters or RCV start data) and its
message; a period that already holds a stored read keeps it. Purchase
details that the SII is still preparing (pending documents) are reported
as unavailable and leave the read partial.

For a daily read, a user who can edit companies enables *Daily SII RCV
Read* on the company (Chilean DTE tab) and selects the *RCV Read User*,
an active DTE Manager of that company, recorded as the requester. The
*SII RCV: Schedule Daily Reads* scheduled action then queues, once per
Chilean day, the purchases and sales of the current and previous month.

The certificate and its private key are written only to a private
temporary directory (files readable by the Odoo user only) for the
duration of the read and removed afterwards; they are never logged.
Requests use bounded timeouts, and a rate-limited or failing query is
retried once.

To import a CSV, a DTE Manager opens *Import CSV*, selects the company,
operation (purchases or sales), year and month, and uploads the file.
The CSV can use commas or semicolons and UTF-8 or Latin-1 encoding.
These columns are required: ``tipo_documento``, ``rut_contraparte``,
``folio``, ``fecha``, ``neto``, ``exento``, ``iva`` and ``total``;
``razon_social``, ``iva_no_recuperable`` and ``iva_uso_comun`` are
optional. The headers of the detail files downloaded from the SII RCV
portal (``Tipo Doc``, ``RUT Proveedor`` or ``Rut cliente``, ``Folio``,
``Fecha Docto``, ``Monto Neto``, ``Monto Exento``,
``Monto IVA Recuperable`` or ``Monto IVA``, ``Monto Total``...) are
accepted as well.

The whole file is rejected when it is empty, larger than 10 MB or lacks
a required column. A row with an invalid document type, folio, RUT, date
or amount, or a document repeated in the file, is not imported: it is
listed on the resulting read, which is marked as partial (failed when no
row is valid). The imported file and its SHA-256 are kept on the read.

Each read reconciles its documents when it is stored: the scheduled SII
read with superuser rights, a CSV import with the rights of the DTE
Manager who imports it. Without access to journal entries the documents
stay pending. The documents list can be filtered by reconciliation state
(missing in accounting, differences, indeterminate, matched). On a
document, the *Reconciliation* group shows the linked journal entry, the
reason code, the message and the VAT comparison; the *Candidate Entries*
tab lists the entries with its document type, folio and RUT, and the
*Reconciliation Log* tab lists every decision.

A DTE Manager with access to invoices can:

- reconcile again a document (*Reconcile*), the selected documents
  (action *Reconcile* of the list) or a whole period (*Reconcile* on the
  period);
- link a document to one of its candidate entries (*Link Entry*), for
  example when several entries share its identity, explaining the review
  behind it. The link is compared at once and later reconciliations keep
  it while the entry remains a candidate;
- unlink a document (*Unlink Entry*), with a reason. Later
  reconciliations do not link it again until it is linked manually.

On vendor bills and customer invoices, the *RCV State* tells whether the
last read of the period reports the document (reported, not reported,
RCV incomplete), and the *RCV* button opens the RCV documents linked to
it. The filters *Not Reported in the RCV*, *RCV Incomplete* and
*Reported in the RCV* are available on the invoice lists. The *RCV Book*
report is printed from the period.

A purchase that declares zero VAT in the RCV but leaves an unexplained
residual in its total stays in manual review: the RCV alone does not
explain it.

Records are restricted to the companies the user works in. DTE users can
read the register and its reconciliation; DTE Managers can also read it
from the SII, import it, reconcile it and link or unlink documents.
