Issues the Chilean electronic settlement invoice (DTE 43,
*liquidación-factura electrónica*). A commission agent or consignee
(*mandatario*, *comisionista*) issues it to its principal (*mandante*)
to settle the sales or purchases made on the principal's behalf and the
agent's commissions and other charges.

**Accounting model.** The document is issued from a posted vendor bill
(``in_invoice``) of the principal, as the purchase invoice DTE 46 is.
For the agent the settlement is a purchase of the settled operations
from the principal, offset by the commission it charges, and the bill
total is exactly what the agent owes the principal:

- settled operations are positive lines with the 19% purchase VAT (or no
  VAT for exempt operations) and the SII type of the document that
  supported each operation (*Settled Document Type*, ``TpoDocLiq``: 33,
  39, 99, ...);
- each commission or other charge is a negative line flagged as an
  *Agent Charge* (``C`` commission or ``O`` other charge) with the 19%
  sales VAT, which is the agent's own output VAT, or no VAT for an
  exempt charge.

A customer invoice cannot hold this document: its total, the amount
payable to the principal, would be negative.

**XML.** DTE_v10 defines the 43 as its own ``Liquidacion`` payload. Each
``Detalle`` carries ``TpoDocLiq``; one ``Comisiones`` line per charge
follows the references (``TipoMovim``, ``Glosa``, ``ValComNeto``,
``ValComExe``, ``ValComIVA``); ``Totales`` reports ``MntNeto``,
``MntExe``, ``TasaIVA``, ``IVA`` of the settled operations, the
``Comisiones`` totals and ``MntTotal``. Per field 124 of the SII
*Formato Documentos Tributarios Electrónicos* (v2.5, 2026-02):

``MntTotal = MntNeto + MntExe + IVA - ValComNeto - ValComIVA - ValComExe``

``VlrPagar`` is optional and not emitted. The folio, TED, signature and
the SII submission in Demo, Certification and Production reuse the
shared lifecycle of ``l10n_cl_dte_account``.

Usage
=====

1. Load a CAF for DTE type 43.
2. Give the principal a valid Chilean RUT, a name, an economic activity
   (*giro*), an address and a commune.
3. Create a vendor bill for the principal with document type 43:

   - one positive line per operation made on the principal's behalf,
     with the 19% purchase VAT or no VAT and its *Settled Document
     Type*;
   - one negative line per commission or other charge, with *Agent
     Charge* set and the 19% sales VAT or no VAT. Its label is the
     ``Glosa`` of the charge.

4. Confirm the bill and press **Issue DTE**. The company numbers, signs
   and sends the DTE 43 to the SII as it does for customer invoices.

Bills without a charge line, with a positive charge, a negative or
discounted operation, a settled operation without its document type,
another currency, additional taxes or withholdings, or a sales VAT on an
operation (or a purchase VAT on a charge) are refused before a folio is
reserved.

Credit and debit notes on a 43, its printed representation and receiving
a 43 from another agent are not supported yet.
