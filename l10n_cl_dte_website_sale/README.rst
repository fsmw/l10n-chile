Adds a **Tax Document** step to the eCommerce checkout of websites whose
company is Chilean. The customer chooses a boleta (final consumer, no
RUT needed) or a factura, which requires a valid Chilean RUT, the
business name, the business activity (giro) and a billing address in
Chile with street and commune. The choice is validated on the server and
stored on the sales order.

When the order is invoiced, manually or by the automatic invoicing of
the shop, the invoice gets the matching DTE type: 39 (or 41 when no line
bears VAT) for a boleta, 33 (or 34) for a factura. Boletas go to the
customer when its partner holds a Chilean RUT and to the generic final
consumer otherwise; facturas go to the partner holding the RUT, which is
created from the checkout data when it does not exist. The existing
**Issue DTE** button and boleta flow then issue the right document.
Other websites and companies are unaffected.

Usage
=====

1. Configure the Chilean company and its DTE journals as usual.
2. In the shop, after the delivery step, the customer picks **Boleta**
   or **Factura**. A factura asks for the RUT, business name and giro;
   the billing address entered at checkout is used as the receiver
   address.
3. Confirm and pay the order. The invoice created from it, by hand or by
   the automatic invoicing setting, already carries the DTE type and
   receiver. Issue it with **Issue DTE**.

The choice and the factura data are shown in the *Other Info* tab of the
sales order. Orders without a choice, for example from express checkout,
are invoiced as boletas. Existing partners are never overwritten by
checkout data: only their empty fiscal fields are completed.
