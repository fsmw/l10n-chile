Lets an invoice refer to an issued Chilean delivery guide (DTE 52).

In the SII references of the invoice, pick the guide: its document type,
folio and date are copied from the issued guide, so the Referencia sent to
the SII cannot drift from it. The guide must belong to the same company
and commercial customer as the invoice. Port of the guide reference of the
SMWLAB module `l10n_cl_edi_delivery_guide`.
