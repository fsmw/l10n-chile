Emails an issued Chilean delivery guide (DTE 52) to the customer once
the SII accepts it, with its XML (the receiver's EnvioDTE in
Certification and Production) and its PDF, the same way
``l10n_cl_dte_delivery`` does for invoices. Production acceptance queues
the delivery automatically; Demo and Certification guides are only sent
with the **Send to Customer** button. The recipient is the customer's
DTE exchange email or its email. Port of the guide delivery of the
SMWLAB module ``l10n_cl_edi_delivery_guide``.

.. IMPORTANT::
   This is an alpha version, the data model and design can change at any time without warning.
   Only for development or testing purpose, do not use in production.
   `More details on development status <https://odoo-community.org/page/development-status>`_

Usage
=====

Issue the guide from ``l10n_cl_dte_dispatch``. In Production the guide
is emailed after SII acceptance; otherwise press **Send to Customer** on
the frozen guide. The delivery state and message stay on the guide.
