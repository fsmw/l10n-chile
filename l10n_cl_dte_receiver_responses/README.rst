This module sends the receiver's own exchange responses for the supplier
DTEs registered by ``l10n_cl_dte_inbox``. Since Law 20.956 the legal
acceptance or claim of a DTE is the SII registry action (ACD, RCD, ERM,
RFP, RFT); the emailed XML files below are the commercial exchange that
the SII format still defines and that suppliers' systems expect.

- **RespuestaDTE with RecepcionEnvio**: answers the reception of a
  received file, with the envelope status (received, schema error,
  signature error, wrong receiver) and the reception status of each DTE
  in it.
- **RespuestaDTE with ResultadoDTE**: the commercial result of each DTE:
  0 accepted, 1 accepted with objections, 2 rejected, with a reason.
- **EnvioRecibos**: one Law 19.983 receipt of goods or services per DTE,
  with the receipt place (Recinto), the signer's RUT and the legal
  declaration.

Every response is signed with the company's signing certificate (XMLDSig
RSA-SHA1 over the referenced element, inclusive C14N), validated against
the official SII schemas bundled in ``static/xsd`` and verified before
it is stored. The exact signed XML is kept as evidence with its SHA-256
and emailed to the supplier's DTE exchange address. Each kind of
response is prepared once per DTE (once per received file for
receptions); emailing it again is an explicit "Resend" of the same XML.

Usage
=====

Signing needs the Certification or Production SII environment and an
active signing profile with a private key for the company.

To answer the reception of a received file, open it in *Incoming DTEs >
Import History* (or open one of its DTEs) and click **Email Reception
Response**.

To send a commercial result or a goods receipt, review the incoming DTE
and click **Email Commercial Result** or **Email Goods Receipt**.
Several DTEs can be answered at once from the list with *Action > Email
Exchange Response*; one response is signed per supplier.

The supplier's address is, in order: the partner's *DTE Exchange Email*
(when ``l10n_cl_dte_delivery`` is installed), the issuer email declared
in the DTE (``CorreoEmisor``) and the partner's email. It can be
corrected on the response before it is sent. The *Exchange Responses*
menu lists every response with its signed XML, SHA-256 and email status;
**Resend** emails the same XML again.

When an ACD, RCD or ERM action is accepted by the SII registry, its form
offers **Email Exchange Response** (ACD: result 0, RCD: result 2, ERM:
receipt). To sign and email it automatically, enable *Email Exchange
Responses After SII Registry* on the company's *Chilean DTE* tab. It is
disabled by default and nothing is emailed automatically without it.
