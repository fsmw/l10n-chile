Issues the Chilean electronic purchase invoice (DTE 46, *factura de
compra electrónica*) from a posted vendor bill. The company, as buyer,
issues the document on behalf of a seller that does not issue invoices
and retains the whole VAT.

The DTE states the company as Emisor and the supplier as Receptor.
Detail lines subject to the retention carry ``CodImpAdic`` 15, and
``Totales`` reports ``MntNeto``, ``MntExe``, ``TasaIVA`` 19, ``IVA``,
one ``ImptoReten`` (``TipoImp`` 15, ``TasaImp`` 19, the retained VAT)
and ``MntTotal``, the amount the buyer pays the seller: net plus exempt
plus VAT minus the retained VAT.

The document reuses the shared issuance lifecycle of
``l10n_cl_dte_account``: CAF folio, TED, XML signature, validation and
the SII submission in Demo, Certification and Production.

This version supports only the total VAT retention (SII code 15 at 19%).
Partial retentions (SII codes 30 to 48), second category withholdings
and additional taxes are refused.

Usage
=====

1. Load a CAF for DTE type 46.
2. Give the supplier a valid Chilean RUT, a name, an address and a
   commune.
3. Create a vendor bill with document type 46. On each taxable line set
   the 19% purchase VAT and exactly one VAT retention:

   - the total VAT withholding of the Chilean chart (``19% WH``, SII
     code 15); or
   - a partial (or product) VAT retention tax that you create yourself,
     because the Chilean chart ships none: a purchase tax, percentage,
     with the negative official rate and the SII code below. Exempt
     lines carry no tax.

4. Confirm the bill and press **Issue DTE**. The company issues and
   signs the DTE 46 and sends it to the SII as it does for customer
   invoices.

Supported retention codes and rates (SII "Formato Documentos Tributarios
Electrónicos" v2.5, 2026-02, section 4):

======== =============== ========
SII code Product         Tax rate
======== =============== ========
15       Total retention -19%
30       Legumes         -10%
31       Wild produce    -19%
32       Cattle          -8%
33       Wood            -8%
34       Wheat           -4%
36       Rice            -10%
37       Hydrobiological -10%
38       Scrap metal     -19%
39       PPA             -19%
41       Construction    -19%
47       Cardboard       -19%
48       Raspberries     -14%
======== =============== ========

Each line states its code in ``CodImpAdic``; the totals carry one
``ImptoReten`` per code with its rate and retained amount, ``IVANoRet``
with the VAT that is not retained (when greater than zero) and
``MntTotal`` = net + exempt + VAT − total retained. Lines with different
codes, including code 15, can be mixed.

Bills with another retention code (35, 40, 42 to 46, 49, the cambio de
sujeto codes 301, 321, ...), a retention whose rate differs from the
official one, two retentions on one line, a second category withholding,
another currency or negative lines are refused before a folio is
reserved.
