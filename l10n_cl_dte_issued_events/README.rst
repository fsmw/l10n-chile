Tracks how receivers answer the DTEs the company issues, without ever
sending a response or writing accounting entries.

- **SII registry events (Law 20.956).** For invoices (33, 34, 43 and 46)
  accepted by SII in Certification or Production, the module lists the
  events of the SII acceptance and claim registry
  (``listarEventosHistDoc``): ACD, ERM, RCD, RFP, RFT, PAG, CED, NCA,
  ENC and any other code SII returns. Each event is stored once per
  document, source, code and event date.
- **Emailed responses.** When the DTE intake mailbox of
  ``l10n_cl_dte_inbox`` receives a ``RespuestaDTE`` (RecepcionEnvio or
  ResultadoDTE) or an ``EnvioRecibos`` (Law 19.983 receipt of goods)
  that refers to one of the company's issued DTEs, its XML signatures
  are verified and the file is kept as evidence linked to the document.
  Files with an invalid signature are refused; verified files that match
  none of our DTEs are kept and flagged.
- **Receiver state.** The document and its invoice show a derived state:
  accepted, goods received, content claimed, partial or total lack of
  goods claimed, paid, assigned, rejected by email, awaiting receiver or
  tacitly accepted once the eight calendar days after SII reception pass
  without a claim. SII registry events take precedence over emailed
  answers. A claim schedules a to-do activity for the invoice
  salesperson.

Usage
=====

Open an issued DTE and go to the **Receiver Responses** tab. A DTE
manager can press **Check Receiver Response** to query the SII registry
at once. The scheduled action *DTE: Check Receiver Responses in the SII
Registry* polls the documents of the legal window every hour, with a
backoff from one hour up to one day, makes a last check when the
eight-day window closes and then stops following the document. It also
stops once a content claim, a total lack of goods claim, or both an
acceptance and a goods receipt are recorded.

Emailed ``RespuestaDTE`` and ``EnvioRecibos`` files are read by the DTE
intake mailbox configured in ``l10n_cl_dte_inbox``. Review them under
**Accounting > DTE Issuance > Operations > Receiver Responses**; use the
*Not Matched* filter to find files that refer to no DTE of the company.
The invoice form shows the receiver state as a badge.
