This module connects the SII purchase and sales register (RCV) of
``l10n_cl_dte_rcv`` with the inbox of received DTEs of
``l10n_cl_dte_inbox`` and its vendor bills of
``l10n_cl_dte_inbox_account``.

- An RCV purchase and the received DTE with the same company, issuer
  RUT, document type and folio are linked when either of them arrives:
  when an RCV read or CSV import stores the purchase, and when the inbox
  registers the DTE (by import or by email). Both forms show the link.
- The RCV reconciliation also considers the vendor bills bound to the
  received DTE, and the signed XML of that DTE, already validated by the
  inbox, proves the VAT of a purchase that the RCV reports with zero
  VAT. Such a document is matched with the reason *Source document
  confirms the VAT*.
- A DTE manager can prepare the vendor bill draft of a purchase missing
  in accounting. When the inbox holds the received DTE, the draft is
  prepared from its signed XML by the incoming DTE vendor bill preview,
  with its lines, taxes, global discounts and additional taxes.
  Otherwise it is prepared from the RCV amounts, one line per VAT
  component, for review.
- Optionally, a daily scheduled action prepares those drafts
  automatically for the companies that enable it.

Drafts are dated in the RCV tax period and are never posted.

Usage
=====

To prepare a vendor bill draft:

1. Open an RCV purchase in *Missing in Accounting* state and click
   *Prepare Draft Bill*.
2. Without a received DTE, review the supplier, the journal and the
   lines proposed from the RCV amounts, with their accounts and taxes.
   When no supplier has the RUT of the document, tick *Create the
   Supplier* and enter its name; a supplier is never created otherwise.
3. With a received DTE, click *Prepare from the Received DTE*: the
   incoming DTE vendor bill preview opens, dated in the RCV period. The
   received DTE must be reviewed and locally validated in the inbox.
4. The draft is linked to the RCV document by the reconciliation.

To prepare the drafts automatically, enable *Automatic RCV Vendor Bill
Drafts* in the SII settings of the company and select the *RCV Draft
User*: an active DTE manager with the *Incoming DTE Vendor Bills* role
and access to the company. Every day, the scheduled action *SII RCV:
Prepare Missing Vendor Bill Drafts* prepares the draft of each
registered purchase missing in accounting, with the rights of that user.
It never creates suppliers and never posts; the reason a draft could not
be prepared is shown on the RCV document.

Use *Find Received DTE* on an RCV purchase, or *Find Received DTEs* on a
selection, to link purchases stored before the module was installed.
