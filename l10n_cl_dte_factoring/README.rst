Assigns ("cesión electrónica", Ley 19.983) an issued Chilean electronic
invoice (DTE 33 or 34) accepted by the SII to a factoring company.

The addon builds the Archivo Electrónico de Cesión (AEC) from the exact
signed DTE bytes kept as evidence by ``l10n_cl_dte_core``, adds the
assignment data (assignor and authorized signer, assignee, amount, last
due date and the sworn declaration), signs the DTECedido, Cesion and AEC
elements with the company certificate, and validates the file against
the official SII schema ``AEC_v10.xsd`` bundled in ``static/xsd``.

The signed AEC is uploaded to the SII Registro de Transferencia de
Créditos (RTC) with the certificate-signed seed/token authentication of
``l10n_cl_dte_gateway``, in Certification (maullin) or Production
(palena). Every upload and status query keeps its request and response
digests and raw evidence. An upload whose answer is lost becomes
*Outcome Unknown* and is never sent again automatically.

Accounting is not changed: the addon records the assignment, the
factoring company and the SII TrackID on the invoice.

Usage
=====

Requirements:

- the invoice is a posted, unpaid customer invoice with DTE type 33 or
  34, issued in Certification or Production and accepted by the SII;
- the company has its RUT, street, city and email, and one active
  signing profile for the SII mode; the certificate holder must be
  authorized by the company in the SII to assign documents;
- the factoring company has a Chilean RUT, street, city and email.

Steps, as a DTE manager:

1. On the invoice press **Assign to Factoring**, select the factoring
   company and review the amount, the last due date and the sworn
   declaration (pre-filled with the SII wording; required when the
   merchandise receipt is not electronic). Save.
2. Press **Sign AEC**. The AEC is signed, validated against
   ``AEC_v10.xsd`` and stored as evidence.
3. Press **Send to SII**. The upload runs as a queue job and stores the
   SII receipt and its TrackID.
4. Press **Check SII Status** until the assignment is *Assigned*
   (``EOK``) or *Rejected*.

If the upload answer is lost, the assignment becomes *Outcome Unknown*.
It is never uploaded again automatically. Press **Check SII Registry**
to ask the SII whether the DTE is recorded as assigned, or check the RTC
portal, and cancel the assignment only when you are sure SII did not
register it.

DTE users can read assignments, evidence and attempts; only DTE managers
act.

Accounting: the addon does not create journal entries. When the
factoring company pays, record the transfer of the receivable yourself,
for example with a journal entry that credits the customer receivable
(reconciled with the invoice) and debits the factoring company
receivable or the bank, plus the factoring cost.
