Received purchase invoices (DTE 46)
===================================

When a buyer issues an electronic purchase invoice (DTE 46) for goods or
services it bought from us, the signed XML arrives in our inbox with the
buyer as issuer and our company as receiver. It documents our own sale.
This bridge books it as a native customer invoice draft built from the
signed XML:

- revenue lines from the XML detail (net amounts);
- sales VAT 19% (SII code 14), declared as our output VAT;
- the VAT the buyer retained in full (``ImptoReten`` code 15) as a
  sale-side "VAT retained by the buyer" tax, so the receivable equals
  ``MntTotal``.

The draft keeps the buyer's folio and document type 46, is linked to the
immutable inbox evidence through the shared binding, and is never
offered for DTE issuance from our side. The inbox acceptance and claim
actions stay available.

Configuration
=============

Configuration
=============

The Chilean chart of accounts has no sale-side VAT retention tax, so the
bridge requires an explicit configuration and refuses to prepare a DTE
46 until it is complete:

1. Create a dedicated sale journal that uses documents for received
   purchase invoices. The folios belong to each buyer's CAF and must not
   collide with the numbering of our own invoices.
2. Choose the sales VAT 19% tax (SII code 14), price excluded.
3. Create a sale tax "VAT retained by the buyer": percent, -19%, SII
   code 15, price excluded, whose invoice tax repartition line posts to
   a balance account such as "VAT retained by third parties" (an asset
   that offsets the declared output VAT).
4. In Accounting settings, under **Incoming Chilean DTE**, select the
   journal and both taxes. The **Incoming DTE Vendor Bill
   Configuration** role is required.

Operators need the **Incoming DTE Vendor Bills** role, and the buyer
must exist as a partner with its valid Chilean RUT and taxpayer type.

Usage
=====

Usage
=====

1. Import the DTE 46 into the inbox and mark it as reviewed after the
   local schema, signature and TED validation succeeds.
2. Click **Prepare Customer Invoice**. Review the buyer, amounts and
   revenue accounts per detail, then **Refresh Preview** and **Confirm
   Draft**.
3. Post the customer invoice natively. Posting re-checks the invoice
   against the signed XML. The **Issue DTE** action is not available for
   it.

The resulting entry for a 1,000 net sale with total retention is:
receivable 1,000 debit, VAT retained by the buyer 190 debit, revenue
1,000 credit and sales VAT 190 credit.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

Roadmap
=======

- Partial VAT retention codes 30 to 48 (``IVANoRet``) are refused.
- Additional taxes on a DTE 46 are refused.
- Only new customer invoice drafts are created; linking an existing
  draft is not supported.
- Folios are unique per journal; two buyers using the same folio need
  separate journals.
